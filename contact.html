Frequently Asked Questions

How much is delivery, and where do you deliver?

We home-deliver everywhere in Bangladesh for a flat ৳100 — Dhaka to Teknaf, one price.

Can I pay cash on delivery?

Yes. Order online and pay in cash when your order reaches you. We'll call to confirm every order first.

How do I place an order?

Add products to your bag, tap Checkout and enter your name, mobile number and address. Then tap Place order — your order is placed right here on the website and you'll see your order number straight away. Pay in cash when it arrives.

How long do Aurora fragrances last?

Aurora fragrances are made with premium-grade, long-lasting formulations. Performance varies by scent and skin, but longevity is the promise our brand is built on.

Browse by scent family — fresh, woody, sweet, floral and more — or by For Men, For Women and Unisex, and read the top, heart and base notes on every product page. Still unsure? Message us and we'll recommend one for your taste and occasion.
