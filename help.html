My orders

Aurora doesn't use customer accounts — there's no password to remember. Every order is placed and confirmed through WhatsApp, so your order history lives in your chat with us.

To check on an order, change it or ask a question, message us on WhatsApp from the number you ordered with.

Message us about an order

Shipping & delivery

  • We deliver to your door anywhere in Bangladesh.
  • Delivery costs a flat ৳100 per order, wherever you are.
  • We call or message to confirm every order before it's sent.

Payment

Pay cash on delivery — you pay the courier when your order arrives. All prices are in Bangladeshi Taka (৳).

Order tracking

After you place your order on WhatsApp, we confirm it with you and keep you updated in the same chat. For the latest status of your parcel, just message us with your name and mobile number.

Track my order on WhatsApp

Returns & exchanges

If there's a problem with your order — a wrong item, or a product that arrived damaged — please message us on WhatsApp as soon as you receive it, with a photo, and we'll help you sort it out.

Privacy policy

  • Your shopping bag is saved only in your own browser on your device.
  • When you check out, your name, mobile number and address are written into a WhatsApp message that you choose to send to us. We use them only to confirm and deliver your order.
  • To make checkout faster next time, your name, number and address are remembered in your browser. Clearing your browser data removes them.
  • If you subscribe to our newsletter, we keep your email address to send you Aurora news. You can ask us to remove it at any time.

Terms & conditions

  • Prices are shown in Bangladeshi Taka (৳) and depend on the size you choose. Delivery is charged separately at a flat ৳100.
  • An order is confirmed once we've confirmed it with you by phone or WhatsApp.
  • Payment is cash on delivery.
  • Product images show Aurora's presentation of each fragrance.
